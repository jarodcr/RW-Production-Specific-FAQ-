Quick Reference Cheat Sheet

Use this while you work. The guide explains the rules; this sheet shows how to apply them.

1 Which Answer?
YES

The clip clearly matches the statement.

Would you bet on it after one more replay?

NO + reason

You can point to what actually happens instead.

Can you finish "No, because actually..."?

NOT SURE + reason

"I don't think so, but I can't really tell."

A hunch is not proof. If you can't point to it, it isn't a No.

FLAG

Clip is broken, black, silent, or the wrong clip.

Never answer Yes / No / Not sure on a broken clip.

🔎 Named entities only: "Is this Tom Hanks?" or "Is this an Audi R8?" Image search the name, compare, move on after 1 min. This is the only place research is allowed.
🧠 Specialist knowledge: Not sure → 3, no research. That's the answer we want. It tells us the question needs different support.
2 Reasons for "No"
# Reason When to Use Example Comment Good Comment
1 Wrong place or direction The thing is there, but position or movement is wrong "Car drives toward the camera", but it's driving away Optional "Car is moving away from the camera."
2 It doesn't happen in the video The event or action is simply not there "Child blows out candles", but there's no cake or candles Not expected
3 It happens, but the description is off You can see the real detail and it contradicts the claim "Orchestral" but it's electronic synth; "two people" but you count five Required "Music is electronic synth, not orchestral."
4 Something else is wrong Clearly wrong but 1-3 don't fit "Dog barks, then door opens", but door opens first Required "Order reversed: door opens before bark."
3 Reasons for "Not Sure"
# Reason When to Use Example Comment Good Comment
1 I can't see or hear well enough Clip too short, dark, blurry, blocked, or too quiet Person holds something, but it's too blurry to tell what Optional "Clip ends before the action finishes."
2 Question doesn't fit this clip The claim is confused or refers to something not there to judge "Second speaker disagrees", but clip is a silent landscape Required "Claim mentions speakers; clip has no people or speech."
3 I can see/hear clearly, but still can't tell Clip is fine; borderline case or knowledge gap Bird in sharp focus, but can't tell if it's a kingfisher Optional "Clear view, but I can't identify the bird species."
4 Something else You're unsure and 1-3 don't fit Clip has two scenes; unclear which one the claim means Required "Two scenes in clip; claim could refer to either."
4 Easy to Mix Up
Torn Between Pick How to Decide
No 3 vs Not sure 1 Not sure 1 Unless you can positively identify the real detail. Something in hand but can't tell what → NS1. Clearly a spoon, not fork → No 3.
Not sure 1 vs Not sure 3 Ask: clip or me? Blurry, dark or quiet clip → 1. Clear clip, but you lack knowledge → 3.
No 2 vs Not sure 2 Does statement fit? Makes sense, just didn't happen → No 2. Doesn't make sense to judge → NS 2.
No 1 vs No 3 Position or not? Left/right, toward/away, above/below → No 1. Color, count, sound, type → No 3.
Not sure 1 vs 🚩 Flag Clip usable? Watchable but detail is hard → NS 1. Black, frozen, silent or wrong → Flag.
Research or not? Named entity only "Is this Homer Simpson?" → search. "Is this a santoku knife?" → no search.
5 Special Question Types
🔎 Named-entity questions are the only place research is allowed. Image search the name and compare to screen. Max 1 minute.
Situation Example Answer Why
Clear match "Is this Homer Simpson?" Bald, yellow, white shirt, blue trousers Yes Matches the known look
Clearly not a match "Is this Tom Hanks?" Very different age and build No Obviously a different person
Clip doesn't show enough "Is this Homer Simpson?" Blurry yellow shape from behind NS → 1 Clip is the problem: blurry, dark, face turned away
Clear but not confident "Is this Tom Hanks?" Looks similar, could be a lookalike NS → 3 Don't guess, people vary at telling faces apart
Specialist knowledge "Is the undercarriage visibly torn open?" NS → 3 No research. Knowledge gap is the answer we want.
6 Comments That Help
⚠️ Required comment reasons: No 3, No 4, Not sure 2, Not sure 4. Write the comment before pressing Enter.
❌ Not helpful
"Wrong"
✅ Helpful
"Ball is blue, not red."
Says what's actually there
❌ Not helpful
"See clip"
✅ Helpful
"I count 4 people, not 2."
Reviewers can check in seconds
❌ Not helpful
"Weird question"
✅ Helpful
"Claim refers to an article; this is a video."
Explains what's off about the question
❌ Not helpful
"Other"
✅ Helpful
"Events happen in reverse order."
Required reasons need actionable info
